        How it works
        
      
        The flight is made backwards: Earthfall flies away from the person and up through the clouds, then plays it in reverse. The view from orbit is painted afterwards from the landscape that flight reveals, so the two match. Full technical graph →
        
      
      
        
        
        How LTX is used
        
        
        Every moving frame is LTX 2.5: the 22B distilled model (int8) with its Gemma text encoder, on one RTX 4090. One seed per stage, no picking between takes. These are the LTX features each film relies on.
        
        
          
          
        1. LTXVImgToVideoInplace · LTXVPreprocess
          Living portrait
          Image to video from the FLUX 3 landing photo. The camera cranes down to the person, the wind moves grass, hair and clothes, and the person does the ending you chose.
          2. 
          
        2. Generated as a retreat
          Made backwards
          LTX flies away from the person, up over the landmark and into cloud. Its first frames follow the portrait clip in reverse, so played backwards the dive lands exactly on the living portrait.
          3. 
          
        3. LTXVAddGuide · LTXVCropGuides
          Keyframe and video guides
          Image pins at chosen frames (the landmark view, a high aerial) at partial strength, plus moving clips as guides: a still alone stalls the camera, a moving guide keeps it flying.
          4. 
          
        4. Prompt Relay (Kijai, pinned)
          Timed prompts
          Each stretch of the 193-frame flight gets its own prompt: orbit, clouds, high aerial, approach, settle. LTX follows one camera move through all five.
          5. 
          
        5. Layout To Render IC-LoRA
          Camera control from a proxy
          A simple globe camera proxy, followed at 0.3, steers the opening from orbit into cloud. LTX invents the clouds and meets the flight’s own frames inside them.
          6. 
          
        6. LTX 2.5 latent upscaler ×2
          Two-pass render
          A first pass at 1280×736, then the spatial upscaler and a refine pass to 2560×1472.
          7. 
          
        7. ManualSigmas · SamplerCustomAdvanced
          Distilled schedule
          The distilled model’s short fixed schedule, with ComfyUI’s --deterministic, so a saved seed rebuilds the same clip.
          8. 
          
        8. Frame-exact joins
          No crossfades
          Clips meet on whole frames: the opening inside the cloud, the flight into the portrait. Nothing is blended or interpolated.

        Limits
        What is generated
        Everything except your portrait and the landmark photos is generated: the landing photo, the flight, the land around the landmark and the view from orbit. Beyond the landmark itself the geography is invented. It is not a map.
        What the checks do
        A local Qwen3.5 model sketches the regional layout and checks each stage. When a check fails or is unsure, the build stops and keeps the take. Small models miss things, so watch the film before sharing it. Endings marked “trial” fail more often.
