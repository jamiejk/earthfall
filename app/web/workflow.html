What runs

Create prepares the landing photo and moving portrait, generates the flight, then paints the view from orbit: an overhead texture, three FLUX Fill expansions, the curved-Earth projection and the orbital finish. LTX then generates a new opening that joins the flight inside the cloud.

The Bamburgh film is 195 frames at 24 fps (8.1 s). The new opening supplies frames 0–39; frames 40–194 are the original flight, unchanged.

What is checked

Qwen3.5 picks the frame below the clouds, sketches the land and water layout, and checks each expansion, the orbital image and the finished film. A failed or unsure check stops the build. These checks do not guarantee motion quality or geographic accuracy.

The flight code and settings are frozen copies of the accepted run, verified by hash before each build.
